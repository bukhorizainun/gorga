IIHow Gorga works

The applet, the student, and a good question.

1

Work in the applet

The teacher's GeoGebra activity, unchanged. Gorga reads the markers and the answer boxes directly from the applet.

2

Explain in your words

Students write short, informal answers with words and numbers. Poda reads which strategy they used.

3

One question back

Poda picks the teacher's scaffold level and asks one question, until the student finds the reasoning alone.

IVThe teacher's framework

Help rises when stuck, fades on success.

L1 · Probe

Probe

An open question. Nothing is pointed out yet.

L2 · Point

Point

One feature of the applet, without saying what it means.

L3 · Focus

Focus

One smaller sub-question, without the procedure.

L4 · Revoice & fade

Revoice, then fade

The student explains; Poda confirms with no new explanation.

Framework: van de Pol, Volman & Beishuizen (2010); van de Pol & Elbers (2013); Wood, Bruner & Ross (1976).

VSafe by design

Every AI sentence is checked first.

No give-aways

Numbers the student has not found yet may not appear in a reply.

Content, not labels

A reply labelled “L4” that still splits the question is rejected.

Always a fallback

If the AI breaks a rule, the student gets a sentence the teacher approved.

Stays on topic
do you like playing Free Fire?
Let's focus on the task first: have you tried moving Sada in the applet to see where she is after 2 seconds?
